Our Story

Started with four people and a vision

Cynoca Tech was born from the vision of four developers who shared a simple belief: technology should empower, not overwhelm. Starting out as friends with diverse skills in software engineering, product design, cloud systems, and business strategy, they came together with a common goal — to build tools that make collaboration seamless and accessible

We kept the same approach as we grew: define the point, connect the edges, resolve the surface. Every project starts as a single constraint and gains dimension through careful, deliberate work. Nothing ships until the structure holds.

Today we're a team of 12 engineers, designers, and strategists working with ambitious founders and established studios who need software that outlasts the trend it launched with.

What We Stand For

Our values, in practice

These aren't aspirational statements. They're the actual criteria we use to make decisions every day.

01

Precision

We'd rather ship one thing that works perfectly than three things that work mostly. Precision is a discipline, not a personality trait.

02

Candor

We tell clients what we actually think, not what they want to hear. The best partnerships are built on honest conversations, even the uncomfortable ones.

03

Craft

The details matter. We care about the quality of the code, the clarity of the interface, and the elegance of the architecture — in equal measure.

04

Longevity

We optimize for the product you'll have in year three, not just launch day. Short-term shortcuts create long-term debt — we don't take them.
